BRD: Online Account Opening (Retail Banking)

Business Goal:
Enable customers to open a retail savings account online within 10 minutes.

Scope:
- Web channel only
- New-to-bank customers
- Savings account product

Actors:
- Customer
- Bank KYC Service
- Core Banking System

High-Level Flow:
1. Customer starts account opening and provides personal details (name, DOB, address, phone, email).
2. Customer uploads ID proof and address proof.
3. System performs KYC verification.
4. If KYC is successful, customer selects account type and submits application.
5. System creates account in core banking and displays account number.
6. Customer receives confirmation email and SMS.

Business Rules:
- Applicant must be 18+ years old.
- KYC must be successful to proceed.
- If KYC fails, show error and allow retry once.
- If second KYC attempt fails, application is rejected.
- Email and phone are mandatory.
- OTP must be verified for phone.

Non-Functional Requirements:
- Each step should complete within 3 seconds.
- PII should be masked in logs.

Edge Cases:
- OTP mismatch.
- KYC service timeout.
- Duplicate application for same ID.
